bmad-dash — five dark directions

The same dashboard fragment in five palettes. All use Material 3 tonal elevation, so tiles separate from the ground by surface tone rather than shadow. All body text clears 4.5:1 on its own surface; accents and focus rings clear 3:1.

Real content throughout — the activity feed, the core-artifact tiles, the four signal states, and a focused control so you can judge the focus ring. Pick a number, or say what to pull from one into another.
